# Compliance Checklist

This checklist is a pre-review aid, not legal advice or a substitute for the insurer or intermediary's approval process. Regulatory requirements and platform policies change; use the latest internal policy and approved materials.

## Evidence Levels

- `A / 已提供依据`: directly supported by an approved product document, insurer notice, official regulation, or clearly cited authoritative source.
- `B / 一般常识待引用`: educational statement that may be usable after a source or careful wording is added.
- `C / 未核验`: product-specific, numerical, case-specific, or outcome claim without evidence. Do not present as fact.

## Block or Rewrite When Content Includes

- Guaranteed returns, guaranteed claims, guaranteed approval, or guaranteed customer acquisition
- Absolute language such as “最好”, “100%”, “稳赚”, “全赔”, “永久”, “绝不拒赔” without a narrowly applicable approved basis
- A comparison that omits material exclusions, eligibility limits, waiting periods, renewal terms, or costs
- A product benefit, premium, rate, coverage amount, claim example, or tax statement not supported by current approved material
- “以理财代替保险”, “保险等于存款”, or other misleading category comparisons
- Fabricated customer stories, copied testimonials, fake urgency, or pressure to buy immediately
- Advice that appears personalized without collecting and assessing necessary information
- Requests for personal health, identity, or financial information in a public comment
- An AI avatar, synthetic voice, or generated case presented as a real person or real event without disclosure

## Required Review Questions

1. Who is the publisher and under which insurer or intermediary is the content issued?
2. Is the topic education, general marketing, or product sales?
3. Is every material product claim traceable to a current approved source?
4. Are exclusions, conditions, eligibility, waiting period, renewal, and uncertainty represented fairly?
5. Does the CTA promise an outcome or create improper urgency?
6. Does the content expose personal information or invite it in an insecure channel?
7. Has the responsible compliance reviewer approved the final version?

## Output Status

- `可进入人工复核`: no obvious blocking issue, but publication approval is still required.
- `需要补充材料`: one or more material claims need current source documents or a clearer scope.
- `不建议发布`: blocking risk remains, or the requested framing depends on deception, pressure, or unsupported promises.

## Safer Rewrite Patterns

- Replace “这个产品一定赔” with “是否赔付需要以合同约定、事故事实和理赔审核为准”。
- Replace “收益比银行高很多” with “具体利益演示和实际结果存在条件与不确定性，应以批准材料和合同为准”。
- Replace “现在不买就亏” with “如果你正在做家庭保障规划，可以先从预算、责任和期限三个问题开始梳理”。
- Replace a public request for medical details with “涉及个人健康信息时，请通过机构认可的安全渠道咨询”。
