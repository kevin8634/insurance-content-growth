# Content Workflow

## 1. Minimum Brief

Collect or infer these fields:

- Persona: agent, broker, team leader, or institutional account
- Audience: age or life stage, occupation, family or business context
- Platform: video account, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat, or other
- Topic: insurance education, risk awareness, claims process, family planning, or product explanation
- Objective: awareness, trust, consultation, appointment, or existing-customer follow-up
- Voice: professional, conversational, story-led, concise, or local
- Source material: approved product brochure, terms, insurer FAQ, official public data, or none
- Resonance scene: a concrete life moment, target person's concern, and desired next action

If no source material is supplied, stay at general education level and do not name a product or state a product-specific conclusion.

## 2. Content Modes

### Weekly Plan

Create 5-7 topics. Balance education, common misconceptions, scenario stories, process guidance, and soft consultation prompts. Avoid making every post a product pitch.

Each topic needs a hook, audience problem, one central idea, format, CTA, and risk level.

### Single Short Video

Default structure:

1. Scene hook: a concrete person, moment, and action that the audience can recognize.
2. Emotional tension: name the hesitation or concern without manufacturing fear.
3. Risk gap: define the one insurance question hidden inside the scene.
4. Explanation: one concept in plain language.
5. Boundary: what the explanation does not cover and which source controls.
6. CTA: invite a question, checklist request, or needs conversation.

Keep the spoken script around 120-220 Chinese characters for 30-60 seconds unless the user requests another duration.

### Digital-Human Production Pack

When the user wants to use Jianying's digital human and Doubao for inserts, do not output only a script. Produce four linked layers:

1. Digital-human narration: one factual, conversational script that can be pasted into Jianying.
2. Insert prompts: independent 15-second Doubao prompts, each with subject, action, camera, lighting, mood, continuity, and negative prompts.
3. Timeline map: the exact narration sentence or time range where each insert appears.
4. Editing plan: opening hook, insert points, subtitles, transitions, sound effects, music level, cover, CTA, and final disclosure.

Use no more insert prompts than the user's available daily generation budget. For a five-clip budget, prefer five strong inserts over many weak variations. Each insert must work without relying on text rendered inside the generated video; add text later in Jianying.

Generated inserts should illustrate a concept, not prove an insurance fact. Avoid invented hospital outcomes, fake policy documents, real insurer logos, identifiable customer stories, exact money scenes, or visuals that imply a claim is guaranteed.

### Platform Repurpose

Preserve the factual core while changing the presentation:

- Video account: spoken, direct, one idea, subtitle-friendly sentences.
- Xiaohongshu: searchable title, scenario framing, structured body, useful checklist.
- WeChat: context and trust, less aggressive hook, clearer source notes.
- Douyin: shorter opening, stronger visual rhythm, no deceptive urgency.

Do not mechanically copy a claim across platforms when platform rules or audience expectations differ.

### Lead-Conversion Reply

Provide a helpful first reply, then ask at most one qualification question. Do not request sensitive health, identity, or financial details in public comments. Move sensitive information to an authorized and secure channel controlled by the institution.

## 3. Beginner-Friendly Production

For a normal video, give:

- What to say
- Where to pause
- What text to place on screen
- Whether a talking head, voice-over, or image-led format is appropriate
- A simple CTA

For a digital-human production pack, additionally give:

- Jianying paste-ready narration
- Digital-human tone, pace, avatar framing, and pronunciation notes
- Doubao prompts numbered C1-C5 or fewer
- Insert-to-narration mapping
- A final assembly order and export checklist

Offer an off-camera or voice-over alternative when the user is uncomfortable appearing on camera. Do not imply that an AI avatar or voice clone is the user's real-time presence without disclosure where applicable.

## 4. Quality Bar

Before returning content, check:

- One central idea per piece
- The first sentence identifies a real audience problem through a concrete scene
- The scene is not presented as a real customer case unless the user has authorization and evidence
- No unsupported product or outcome claim
- No fear-based pressure, fabricated case, or absolute promise
- The CTA is specific and low friction
- The script can be read naturally by a beginner
